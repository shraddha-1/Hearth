How to play

  • Build: Pick a house on the left, then click the grass. R rotates before you place.
  • Move: Drag any house, plant, pond, well or farm. Red means there is no room.
  • Grow: Click a roof to add a storey, or use + / −.
  • Name & colour: Select a house to rename it and change walls, timber, roof, shutters and door.
  • Plants & nature: The Plants and Nature tabs add trees, flowers, ponds, wells and farms.
  • Camera: Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, double-click a house to fly in.
  • Time of day: Night, Dusk, Gold, Day or the slider, top right.
  • Village centre: The Houses tab ends with a central barn and a market square with merchant stalls.
  • Menu & names: Hide or show the build menu with the button (or B). Name labels are off by default; turn them on with Names.
  • Remove: Select something and press Delete or the Remove button.
